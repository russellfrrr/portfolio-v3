I wanted this version of my portfolio to feel less like a template and more like a small interface for my work.

<Callout>
The goal was not to make everything louder. The goal was to make every section feel intentional.
</Callout>

I started with a simple direction: cream, oxblood, black, and clean information. From there, every section had to earn its place.

<Highlight>Good design is not decoration. It is decision-making.</Highlight>

This blog system is also part of that direction. I want a writing setup that stays close to the codebase, easy to edit, and flexible enough for notes, lessons, and build logs.
